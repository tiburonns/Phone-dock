# Phone Dock — Aurora identity

Created with the built-in image generation tool (not the API/CLI fallback).

## Deliverables

- AppIcon-Source.png: master square mark, shared by Mac and iPhone.
- Wordmark.png: horizontal logo with transparent background.
- The previous CocoaLift-AppIcon-Source.png remains in the parent folder as an archive.

The app icon exports are generated by script/generate_app_icons.swift. iOS uses an opaque square; macOS uses a rounded mask and transparent exterior margin. The in-app BrandIcon image is kept in both asset catalogs. UI text stays native for accessibility and light/dark contrast.

## Prompts

### Initial concept
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production square app icon for Phone Dock, an independent iPhone remote-control deck for Mac. Create one original minimal, memorable symbol: a bold ivory rounded upright phone outline interlocking with a short horizontal dock tray, together subtly suggesting a lowercase d; one small mint rounded tile sits at the upper-right of the phone as a connection/action cue. Exceptionally simple strong silhouette, thick consistent strokes, generous negative space, visible at 32 pixels. Palette: rich aurora violet #6650D8, lavender #B6A7FF, mint #56BDA8, ivory #F7F7FC. Backdrop: full-bleed opaque square violet with an extremely subtle violet-to-indigo gradient, no exterior margin, no outer rounded-square frame (OS supplies the icon mask). Symbol occupies central 62 percent. Style: precise geometric flat vector-like identity with very restrained dimensional softness, no glossy 3D, no realism. No text, no letters as explicit text, no existing brand symbols, no watermark, no presentation board, only the single finished icon. 1024 by 1024.

### Final app icon
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this Phone Dock app icon. Preserve exactly the central white phone-in-dock symbol and mint tile. Replace the entire fuzzy violet halo and all transparent exterior pixels with a perfectly smooth opaque solid violet #6650D8 background covering EVERY pixel right to all FOUR straight square canvas edges. No transparency anywhere. No feathering, no fog, no texture, no black corners, no separate rounded icon frame, no shadows outside the symbol. Reduce the symbol slightly to occupy 62% of square width and keep it centered. Clean flat geometric app icon, no words. The result must be a full-bleed square purple image with a crisp white/mint symbol.

### Companion wordmark
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: horizontal official wordmark for Phone Dock. Use the attached icon as identity reference: preserve the same rounded phone standing in a short horizontal dock tray, with the little mint tile at upper right. Create a clean horizontal logo on genuinely transparent background: at left a compact violet rounded square containing the white phone/dock and mint tile, at right the exact words 'Phone Dock' in deep graphite #232737, bold friendly geometric rounded sans-serif typography. Accurate text, two words only, no tagline. Make symbol and lettering balanced at similar visual height, ample padding, landscape composition about 3:1. Flat crisp professional design, no shadows or glow, no mockup, no watermark. This is a companion wordmark to the reference app icon, not a new concept.
